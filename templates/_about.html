About Data Potluck

Data Potluck is a platform for crowdsourced data projects. It's one central place to collect contributions from a community, manage and review them, and export the verified data for training. We're still building it.

We're building a speech-to-speech model that translates between Dinka and English. Training a model for this task takes a lot of recordings, which is why we're asking for your help. Together we can create a dataset for our community that can be used for many technologies, like the one we're building.

You'll see a sentence in English. Record yourself saying it in Dinka, then type what you said. That's one contribution. Do as many as you like.

  • Recordings are stored in private cloud storage. Your name, email, and transcriptions are stored in our database.
  • Only the people running the project can listen to individual recordings while we review them.
  • You keep the rights to what you record. By contributing, you give us permission to use it for the purposes described here.
  • When the project is complete, we plan to release the dataset for free, for anyone to use with credit, for non-commercial work. That release includes the recordings, so your voice will be public at that point.
  • Companies that want to use it commercially will have to pay. We intend to share that money with the people who contributed, which is why we ask for your name and email. We can't promise it will happen or how much it will be.
  • You can ask us to remove your data any time at support@datapotluck.com. We'll take it out of the project and out of future releases. We can't take back copies of a dataset that's already been released.

Data Potluck is run by Maria Clement and Billy Clement. Questions? Write to support@datapotluck.com.

We'd like to open Data Potluck to other projects, especially ones building technology that gives back to the communities the data comes from. We're not ready for that yet, but we'd like to hear what you have in mind. Write to us at support@datapotluck.com.
